Word Search Solver

Find every word. In every direction.

How to use the solver ▾
  1. Paste your puzzle letters into the first box.
  2. Put each row of letters on a separate line.
  3. Paste the words you need to find into the second box.
  4. Separate words using new lines or commas.
  5. Tap SOLVE WORD SEARCH.
  6. Tap a found word to highlight its location.

The solver checks horizontal, vertical, diagonal, backwards, and upside-down directions.
