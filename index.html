Word Search Solver

Find every word. In every direction.

How to use the solver
  1. Paste your puzzle letters into the first box, one row per line.
  2. Paste the words to find into the second box, separated by new lines or commas.
  3. Tap SOLVE WORD SEARCH.
  4. Tap a found word to highlight its location.

The solver checks horizontal, vertical, diagonal, and backwards directions. If your puzzle pasted as one long line, enter the grid width below the puzzle box.
